Gummy Galaxy is not just luck, it's all about combo and moves. Here are 5 real tips that worked for me.

1. Always Start From Bottom

Most players make mistake of matching from top. If you match from bottom, upper candies auto fall and create chain combo.

2. Save Your Shuffle

Don't use shuffle at start. Use it only when you see no moves left. It can give you a galaxy combo.

3. Focus on Target, Not Score

Game gives you 25 moves. Don't waste them for small 3-match. Try for 4 or 5 candy match.

4. Hint is Free, Use It

If you are stuck more than 10 seconds, just use hint button. It shows best move.

5. Play Daily for Bonus

Every 5th level gives you 50 extra moves. So don't skip levels.

Final Word: Just practice these 5 tips and you will cross 5000 easily. Good luck!
