What Is Gummy Galaxy?

Gummy Galaxy is one of the most addictive and colourful arcade space adventure games of 2026. Imagine a universe where planets are made of jelly beans, stars are made of gummy bears, and black holes are made of chocolate. That is Gummy Galaxy. In this game, you become Captain Gummy, a brave little gummy bear astronaut who has to save his galaxy from the evil Sour Worms who want to steal all the sweetness from the universe.

The story begins on Planet Jelly, a beautiful candy planet where everything was sweet and happy. One day, the Sour Worm King attacked with his army of sour candies and stole the Sugar Crystal, the source of all sweetness in the galaxy. Without the Sugar Crystal, all gummy bears started losing their colour and taste. Captain Gummy decided to take his small candy spaceship and travel across the entire Gummy Galaxy to get the crystal back. Your mission is to help him.

Unlike other boring space games, Gummy Galaxy is full of colours, music, and fun. Every level is a new candy planet. One planet is made of strawberry jelly, another is made of mint chocolate, and another is made of orange gummy. The graphics are so bright and juicy that you will feel like you are inside a candy shop.

Gummy Galaxy is perfect for all ages. Kids love it because of its cute characters and easy controls. Adults love it because it is very challenging in the later levels and it is a great stress buster. How To Play Gummy Galaxy?

Playing Gummy Galaxy is very easy. You do not need to learn any difficult controls. Just follow these simple steps:

  • Step 1: Wait for the 5-second timer above to finish and click on PLAY button.
  • Step 2: Use your finger on mobile or mouse on PC to move your spaceship left and right.
  • Step 3: Shoot the Sour Worms by tapping or auto-fire. Your ship shoots candy lasers automatically.
  • Step 4: Collect Gummy Stars, Sugar Coins, and Power-ups. Avoid Sour Bombs.
  • Step 5: At the end of each level, fight the Boss Worm and win the Sugar Crystal.

Each level has 3 stars to win. If you complete the level without losing any life, you get 3 stars. These stars unlock new planets and new spaceships. There are many spaceships to unlock like Jelly Rocket, Chocolate Cruiser, and the Legendary Rainbow Gummy Ship which is the most powerful ship in the game.

Power-Ups In Gummy Galaxy

Power-ups make the game even more fun. The Rainbow Shield protects you for 10 seconds. The Double Candy Gun doubles your shooting power. The Magnet Star pulls all coins towards you. The Time Freeze stops all enemies for 5 seconds. Use these power-ups wisely, especially in boss fights. Smart players save their power-ups for the last 30 seconds of the level where the boss becomes very angry and fast.

Pro Tips To Get High Score In Gummy Galaxy

If you want to become a top player in Gummy Galaxy, follow these pro tips. First, always focus on collecting stars, not just killing enemies. Stars give you more points. Second, never waste your power-ups in the starting levels. Save them for level 20 and above where the game becomes very hard. Third, upgrade your spaceship daily. Many players forget to upgrade and then they lose in boss fights. Fourth, play the daily bonus level every day. It gives you free coins and a free life.

Fifth, join a clan. Clan members can send you extra lives when you run out. This is very useful when you are stuck on a hard level. Sixth, watch the pattern of the boss. Every boss has a fixed pattern. If you learn the pattern, you can dodge easily. For example, the Strawberry Boss always shoots 3 lasers to the left, then 2 to the right. If you know this, you will never get hit.

Seventh, play with sound on. The game has special sound cues. When you hear a sweet ding sound, it means a hidden power-up is near. Many players play on mute and miss these hidden rewards. Eighth, do not rush. Gummy Galaxy is not a racing game. Slow and steady wins. Move slowly, aim carefully, and you will clear all levels.

Gummy Galaxy - All Planets And Levels Guide

Gummy Galaxy has 10 major candy planets. Planet 1 is Jelly Land for beginners. Planet 2 is Chocolate World where enemies are faster. Planet 3 is Mint Mountain where there is ice and slippery movement. Planet 4 is Strawberry Sea where you fly over water. Planet 5 is Orange Desert where there are sandstorms. Planet 6 is Blueberry Night where you play in the dark with only your ship light. Planet 7 is Cola Cloud where gravity is low. Planet 8 is Candy Castle, the home of the Sour Worm King. Planet 9 is Rainbow Road, the hardest planet. Planet 10 is Secret Sugar Galaxy which only unlocks if you get 3 stars on all previous levels.

Each planet has 20 levels, so total 200 levels. After you finish all 200, you unlock Endless Mode. In Endless Mode, you keep playing until you lose all lives. The world record in Endless Mode is 5,45,000 points made by a player from India. Can you break that record? Try it today.
